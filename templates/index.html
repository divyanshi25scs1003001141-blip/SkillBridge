How it works

Four steps from sign-up to a clear picture of your preparation.

  1. 1

    Set up your profile

    Add your college, year of study and the technology fields you are curious about.

  2. 2

    Check your skills

    Add the skills you already have and take an assessment to see how well you know them.

  3. 3

    Follow a roadmap

    Choose a career path and work through its steps, ticking each one off as you finish it.

  4. 4

    Review your progress

    Check your dashboard, update your goals and download your readiness report.
