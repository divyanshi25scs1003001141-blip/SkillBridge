About SkillBridge

A learning and career readiness platform for college students who want a clear view of their skills and their next steps.

Why it exists

Many students know which career they are interested in but not which skills it needs, or how far along they already are. Advice is scattered across videos, forums and roadmaps that never connect to what you have actually done.

SkillBridge puts it in one place. You record your skills, test your knowledge, follow a roadmap, keep track of your projects and goals, and see your progress in a single dashboard.

Who it is for

Mainly college students, from first-year beginners choosing a direction to final-year students getting ready to apply. You do not need to know which career you want. You can start with a single assessment and explore from there.

How it treats your data

Every account only ever sees its own records. Passwords are stored as secure hashes, never as plain text. Scores, skill gaps and reports are worked out with plain, readable rules, so there are no paid AI services and no hidden scoring.

What it does not do

SkillBridge shows your learning progress and career preparation. It does not predict whether you will be selected for a job, and it never guarantees one. Treat it as a way to see what to work on next.

How it is built

Python and Flask on the server, SQLite for storage, and HTML, CSS and plain JavaScript in the browser. Free tools only.
