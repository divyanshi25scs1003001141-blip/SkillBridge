How the score is built

  • 25 Structure — contact details and the expected sections
  • 25 Skills match — job skills that also appear in the resume
  • 20 Keyword coverage — important words from the advert
  • 15 Job relevance — those words inside projects and experience
  • 15 Content quality — length, bullets, action verbs, summary

Text extraction cannot see every visual problem, so the formatting checks only cover what can be read from the document.
